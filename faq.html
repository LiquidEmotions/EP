FAQ and policies

Authenticity

Are your perfumes authentic?

Yes. Decants are drawn by syringe from genuine bottles and sealed by hand. Brand new bottles are sealed and in their box (BNIB). Testers and vials are listed as exactly what they are.

What is the difference between BNIB, tester and vial?

BNIB is a sealed pack with its box. A tester is the bottle a brand supplies for testing; each listing says whether it has a box and notes its condition. A vial is a small official sample. More on the About page.

Delivery and shipping

How long does delivery take?

Most orders reach you in about 4 to 7 days. We dispatch first, then it travels to you. Open any perfume and use Check delivery to your pincode to see dates for your area. We confirm exact timing on WhatsApp.

What are the shipping charges?

Decants: ₹55 in Delhi NCR and ₹75 in the rest of India, free on 4 or more items. Shipping is free on any order with a brand new bottle, tester, vial or partial bottle.

Orders and payment

How can I pay?

UPI, or card through Razorpay. EMI is available on eligible credit cards for orders of ₹3,000 and above.

How do I know my order is confirmed?

Your order gets an EP order number, and we confirm it with you on WhatsApp.

How do I track my order?

Use Track your order with your order number and the phone number you ordered with. If you gave an email, we also send a confirmation and a shipping update there.

What if something sells out while I am ordering?

Stock is limited, so two people can sometimes order the last one at the same time. If that happens, we tell you on WhatsApp straight away.

Problems with an order

What if my order arrives damaged or is not what I ordered?

Message us on WhatsApp with photos as soon as it arrives and we will look into it and sort it out with you. Please keep the packaging until then.

Can I return a perfume?

Because decants are filled to order and opened perfume cannot be resold, we do not take returns on them. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp and we will help.
