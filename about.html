About Enigma Perfumes

Enigma Perfumes sells perfume decants, sealed BNIB bottles, testers and vials, each listed with its details and its condition.

What the labels mean

Decant

Decants from 3ml

A small amount of a perfume from a genuine bottle, decanted by syringe and sealed by hand, so you can try it before buying the bottle.

BNIB

Brand new in box

A sealed pack, with its box. Nothing has been opened.

Tester

Tester perfume

The perfume a brand supplies for testing. It can come with or without a box. Every tester listing says whether it has a box and notes its condition, such as scuffs or fill level.

Vial

Sample vial

A small vial of a perfume, so you can try it before buying a full bottle. The size is on every listing.

Delivery

Most orders reach you in about 4 to 7 days. We dispatch your order first, then it travels to you.

We confirm the exact timing with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

How stock works

Stock is limited. When a perfume sells out, its listing shows Sold out.

Because stock is limited, two people can sometimes order the last perfume at the same time. If that happens, we'll tell you on WhatsApp straight away.
