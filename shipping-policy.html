Shipping policy

Delivery time

We dispatch your order first, then it travels to you. Most orders reach you in about 4 to 7 days. Open any perfume and use Check delivery to your pincode to see the dates for your area.

Shipping charges

  • Decants: ₹55 in Delhi NCR and ₹75 in the rest of India.
  • Free shipping when your order has 4 or more items.
  • Free shipping on any order with a brand new bottle, tester, vial or partial bottle.

Tracking

Once your order ships, you can follow it on Track your order with your order number and phone number. If we have your email, we also send a shipping update there.

Packaging

Decants are filled by syringe and sealed by hand. Bottles ship in their box with protective packing.

Questions about delivery? Contact us.
