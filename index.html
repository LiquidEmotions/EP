Questions

What does BNIB mean?

Brand new in box. The perfume is sealed and comes with its box.

What is a vial?

A small sample vial of a perfume, so you can try it before buying a full bottle. The size is on every listing.

What is a tester?

The perfume a brand supplies for testing. It can come with or without a box, and each tester listing says which, plus its condition.

How do I order?

Add perfumes to your cart and check out. Your order opens in WhatsApp, and we confirm availability and next steps there.

What if a perfume sells out while I'm ordering?

Stock is limited, so this can happen. If it does, we'll tell you on WhatsApp straight away.

How long will delivery take?

About 4 to 7 days from the day you order. We dispatch first, and we confirm the exact timing on WhatsApp.

Are there shipping charges?

No. Shipping is free on every order.
